This page explains what information the Your Church Name website (example.org) collects and how it is used. We keep as little as we can, and we never sell or share it for advertising.

Visitors

  • Browsing the site needs no account. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser remembers your English/Español choice on your own device.
  • Forms, RSVPs and polls save only what you type in (for example your name and number of guests). Church administrators use it to plan events, and it is not shared outside the church.
  • Watching live: our live stream plays through YouTube, which applies Google's privacy policy to the video player.

Church administrators

  • Administrator accounts (by invitation only) store a name, email address and role, used to sign in and manage the site.
  • Live Studio and YouTube: when an administrator connects the church's YouTube channel, the site receives permission to manage that channel's live broadcasts. It uses this only to create, start and end the church's own live broadcasts and to show and reply to their live chat. The permission is stored securely on our server, is never shown in a browser, and can be removed at any time from Live Studio (Disconnect) or from the Google account's security settings. The site does not read, change or delete any other YouTube content.
  • Live Studio and Facebook: when an administrator connects the church's Facebook Page, the site receives permission to publish live videos on that Page. It uses this only to start and end the church's own live videos and to show and reply to comments on them. It does not read private messages, post anything else, or access personal profiles. The permission can be removed at any time from Live Studio (Disconnect) or in Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
  • Camera, microphone and screen are used only while Live Studio is open, only on the computer running it, and only to make the live stream.

Where information is kept

The site runs on Google Firebase (United States). Information is kept as long as it is useful to the church and then deleted. To ask for your information to be removed, contact us below.
